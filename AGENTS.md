# Guidelines for this site

- This is Xinning Gui's personal site. Pushing to `main` publishes it. A specific content change she asks for counts as approval to make and publish it; design changes need her approval first.
- Never invent publications, authors, venues, awards, dates, students, or roles. Her CV is the source of truth for publications and their types; if sources disagree, ask her.
- Never put people's photos in black or dark frames.
- Keep visuals meaningful: a drawing must show the technology or setting the associated text is about.
- Keep the muted (Morandi) palette and plain, specific wording.
- Run `pnpm build` after every change; check desktop and phone widths and accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA) for any visual change.
